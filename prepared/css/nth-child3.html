Classic Tomato Soup

A simple, comforting, and delicious homemade tomato soup.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 large Onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 800g Canned Tomatoes
  • 500ml Vegetable Broth
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • Salt and Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add chopped onion and cook until soft. Add garlic and cook for 1 more minute.
  3. Pour in the canned tomatoes and vegetable broth. Add sugar.
  4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and let it simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Carefully blend the soup until smooth.
  6. Season with salt and pepper. Serve hot.

Nutrition Facts

Nutrient Amount
Calories 150 kcal
Protein 3g
Carbs 20g
Fat 7g
